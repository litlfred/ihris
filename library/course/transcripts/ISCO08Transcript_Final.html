ISCO Codes (video transcript)

iHRIS Administrator - Level I, © IntraHealth International, CC BY 4.0. Originally offered at hrhresourcecenter.org/elearning (CapacityPlus). Licence stated by its author, the folio owner, 2026-10-09.

iHRIS supports the International Standard Classification of Occupations Code structure or ISCO Codes as defined by the International Labour Organization. In this video, I will show you how to link a job in iHRIS Manage to the corresponding ISCO Code. Adhering to ISCO Codes enables your organization to standardize its job classifications. That means HR data can be aggregated and compared with other organizations nationally, regionally, or even globally as long as the other organizations also adhere to the same standards. So let’s get started.

First log in as either HR Manager or Administrator. On the home page, click on “Configure System.” Next click on “Administer Database.” Now, under Create Job Structure, click on “Jobs.” Here you see a list of all the jobs that have been entered into iHRIS Manage. You can either select a job to edit or you can add a new job. In this case, let’s add a new job.

Let’s say that my organization has a job titled Nurse 2 that needs to be entered into the system. So under Title, I type: Nurse 2. Under Code, and I’m just making this up, I type: N U R dash 0 2 [Nur – 02]. For the description, I type: Nurse 2 oversees Nurse Aides. Note that when you enter job descriptions, you will probably want to be more detailed than this. For salary grade, I choose “Professional Mid-Level/Managerial” and for Cadre, I choose “Nurse,” and for Classification, I choose “Nurse.” And here you see we have two options for linking to ISCO Codes. We can either link to the 1988 Codes or the 2008 Codes. For this demo, we’ll use the 08 codes just because they’re the most recent. We click on “Select Value.” Notice that the ISCO Codes are organized into major, sub major, minor, and unit groups. I’ll click on “Professionals” and “Health Professionals” and “Nursing and Midwifery professionals” and “Nursing professionals.” Then I click “Confirm” and “Save.” So now I’ve linked my new job title of “Nurse 2” to the corresponding ISCO 2008 Code.
