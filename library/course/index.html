General

This course provides instructions on the basic skill set needed to administer and customize CapacityPlus's human resources information system software, iHRIS Manage and iHRIS Qualify.

The primary audience for this course is people who possess a background in information technology and are interested in installing and maintaining the iHRIS software. Prior experience in software management, databases, or computer programming will be helpful, but is not required. The course begins with a brief overview of human resources and health information systems, then proceeds to the technical content.
