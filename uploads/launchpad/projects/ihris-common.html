Registered 2007-09-10 by IntraHealth Informatics

iHRIS Common includes common page classes, template files and images for the iHRIS Suite of software: iHRIS Manage, iHRIS Qualify and iHRIS Plan. iHRIS Common is free and Open Source software distributed under the GPL.

To join the announcement mailing list for this project, go to http://lists.intrahealth.org/mailman/listinfo/ihris-announce.
To join the developers mailing list for this project, go to http://lists.intrahealth.org/mailman/listinfo/ihris-developers.
